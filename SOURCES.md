# Sources — Tuesday 24 November 2026

Every story on the page was reported on, or led the papers of,
Tuesday 24 November 2026. Figures are as reported.

## Lead

**Factory fire toll rises to 24** — At least 24 people have died
in the fire at a candy factory in Cikande, Banten, Indonesia, with
rescuers still searching for around 30 missing workers. The fire
broke out on Saturday at the Milkita candy factory (PT Fonusa
Agung Mulia).
— Reuters, AP, Lexington Herald Leader, The Times of India, 24 Nov.

## Markets

**Nvidia soars 31% as Huang returns** — Nvidia shares jumped 31%,
their best day since 2003 and a single-day gain of $952bn in
market value, after the board reinstated Jensen Huang as chief
executive, 11 days after he resigned. The Nasdaq rose 6% and the
S&P 500 rose 5%. President Trump said he pressed Nvidia to bring
Huang back.
— CNN, Reuters, Investopedia, MarketWatch, Investors.com, 23–24 Nov.

## Climate

**COP31 ends with no deal** — The climate conference in Antalya,
Turkiye, ended with no agreement on fossil fuels after the longest
COP ever. The final draft dropped every mention of coal, oil and
gas. Talks may be suspended until next year.
— BBC (via WH News), NDTV, 24 Nov.

## War in Europe

**Kyiv region hit** — Russian drone and missile attacks struck
parts of Ukraine's Kyiv region, killing several people and
damaging a residential building in the village of Dymer.
— The Guardian, The Times of India, 24 Nov.
**Warship off Libya** — Ukrainian forces claimed to have sunk a
Russian warship and damaged a cargo vessel off the coast of Libya.
— WH News, 24 Nov.

## Justice

**UK names Rohingya genocide** — Britain formally recognised the
genocide committed against the Rohingya and sanctioned five senior
Myanmar military officers, after the International Court of
Justice ruled on 19 November that Myanmar committed genocide.
London also pledged 5m pounds in humanitarian aid.
— GOV.UK (Foreign Secretary statement, 23 Nov), BBC, 24 Nov.
